======================================== BUSINESS OS - PROJECT VISION
Version: 1.0 ========================================

PROJECT NAME

Business OS (Enterprise ERP)

VISION

Build a secure, scalable, AI-powered Business Operating System that
manages the entire business from one platform.

MISSION

Provide one integrated solution for: - Sales & Billing - Purchase -
Inventory - Warehouse - CRM - Dealer Management - Employee & Payroll -
Branch Management - Marketing - WhatsApp Ordering - QR Payment
Tracking - Reports & Analytics - AI Business Assistant

OBJECTIVES

-   Eliminate manual work
-   Increase business efficiency
-   Real-time business visibility
-   Multi-branch management
-   Offline & online support
-   API-first architecture
-   Enterprise-grade security

TARGET USERS

-   Business Owner
-   Admin
-   Accountant
-   Salesman
-   Branch Manager
-   Warehouse Staff
-   Customer Support
